How to play
  • Motherboard surround the other side's to win; place it by your 4th turn, never your 1st
  • Heatsink one step, or climbs on top and pins what is under it
  • Jumper jumps a straight line to the first empty hex
  • Clock exactly three steps around the edge
  • Packet any distance around the edge
  • FPGA moves as any piece touching it
  • Probe up onto a piece, onto another, then down
  • Crane one step, or lifts a neighbour and sets it down beside itself
  • The hive never splits, and a piece cannot squeeze through a gap between two pieces
